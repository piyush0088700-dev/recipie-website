Chocolate Fudge Cake

An impossibly moist, deeply chocolatey cake crowned with a silky ganache frosting. The kind of cake that silences a room.

60 Minutes
8 Servings
Medium Difficulty
450 Calories

Ingredients

For the Cake

  • 200gAll-purpose flour
  • 200gCaster sugar
  • 60gCocoa powder, unsweetened
  • 2 tspBaking powder
  • ½ tspBaking soda
  • ½ tspSalt
  • 2Large eggs, room temperature
  • 200mlButtermilk
  • 100mlVegetable oil
  • 1 tspVanilla extract
  • 200mlHot strong coffee

For Ganache

  • 200gDark chocolate (70%), chopped
  • 200mlDouble cream
  • 30gUnsalted butter

Prep Time: 20 min Bake Time: 35 min Cool Time: 30 min Total Time: ~1 hr 25 min

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Preheat & Prepare Pans

Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Grease two 20cm round cake tins and line the bases with parchment paper.

2

Mix Dry Ingredients

In a large bowl, sift together flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Whisk to combine evenly.

3

Combine Wet Ingredients

In a separate jug, whisk together eggs, buttermilk, oil, and vanilla extract until smooth.

4

Make the Batter

Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and fold gently until just combined. Add hot coffee and stir until a smooth, pourable batter forms. The batter will be thin — this is correct.

5

Bake the Cakes

Divide batter evenly between tins. Bake for 30–35 minutes until a skewer inserted in the centre comes out clean. Cool in tins for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack.

6

Make the Ganache

Heat cream until just simmering. Pour over chopped chocolate and butter. Let sit 2 minutes, then stir until glossy and smooth. Cool until spreadable, about 20–30 minutes.

7

Frost and Serve

Place one cake layer on a plate. Spread a generous amount of ganache on top. Add second layer and frost the top and sides. Decorate with chocolate shavings or dust with cocoa.
