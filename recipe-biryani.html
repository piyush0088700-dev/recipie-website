Chicken Biryani

Layers of fragrant saffron-tinted basmati rice, slow-cooked spiced chicken, and caramelised onions — a celebration in every bite.

75 Minutes
4 Servings
Medium Difficulty
680 Calories

Ingredients

  • 600gChicken, bone-in pieces
  • 2 cupsBasmati rice, soaked 30 min
  • 2Large onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 cupThick yoghurt
  • 3 tbspGhee or oil
  • 2 tspBiryani masala powder
  • 1 tspGaram masala
  • 1 tspTurmeric powder
  • 1 tspRed chilli powder
  • 1 tbspGinger-garlic paste
  • A pinchSaffron in warm milk
  • HandfulFresh mint & coriander
  • To tasteSalt

Prep Time: 30 min Cook Time: 45 min Total Time: 75 min

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Marinate the Chicken

Mix chicken with yoghurt, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, red chilli powder, biryani masala, and salt. Marinate for at least 30 minutes (overnight is best).

2

Fry the Onions

Heat ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot over medium-high heat. Fry sliced onions until deep golden-brown and crispy, about 15 minutes. Remove half and set aside for garnish.

3

Cook the Chicken

Add marinated chicken to the pot with remaining fried onions. Cook on medium heat, stirring occasionally, until chicken is cooked through and oil separates, about 20 minutes.

4

Par-Cook the Rice

In a separate large pot, boil salted water with whole spices (bay leaf, cloves, cardamom). Add soaked rice and cook until 70% done (grains should still have a bite). Drain.

5

Layer and Dum Cook

Layer par-cooked rice over the chicken. Drizzle saffron milk, sprinkle garam masala, fried onions, and fresh mint on top. Cover tightly, cook on very low heat for 20–25 minutes.

6

Rest and Serve

Let the biryani rest for 5 minutes before opening. Gently fold from the sides to mix layers. Serve with raita and a wedge of lemon.

💡 Chef's Tips

  • The dum (slow steam) technique is what makes biryani truly special — don't lift the lid early.
  • Seal the pot lid with dough for an authentic dum effect.
  • Soaking rice for 30 minutes gives longer, fluffier grains.
  • Caramelised onions (birista) are non-negotiable for authentic flavour.
