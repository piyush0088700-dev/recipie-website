Classic Pasta Carbonara

A Roman masterpiece — silky egg-and-parmesan sauce that clings to every strand of pasta. Simple ingredients, extraordinary results.

25 Minutes
2 Servings
Easy Difficulty
520 Calories

Ingredients

  • 200gSpaghetti or rigatoni
  • 100gPancetta or guanciale, diced
  • 2Large eggs
  • 1Egg yolk (extra)
  • 75gPecorino Romano, grated
  • 25gParmesan, grated
  • 2 clovesGarlic
  • To tasteFreshly cracked black pepper
  • 1 tspSalt (for pasta water)

Prep Time: 10 min Cook Time: 15 min Total Time: 25 min

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Boil the Pasta

Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Add spaghetti and cook until al dente, about 8–10 minutes. Reserve 1 cup of starchy pasta water before draining.

2

Crisp the Pancetta

While pasta cooks, place pancetta and garlic in a cold pan. Cook over medium heat until pancetta is golden and crisp, about 5–6 minutes. Remove garlic. Turn off heat.

3

Make the Egg Sauce

In a bowl, whisk together eggs, extra yolk, most of the grated pecorino and parmesan, and a generous amount of black pepper. Mix until smooth and combined.

4

Combine Off Heat

Add hot drained pasta to the pan with pancetta (heat off). Quickly pour the egg mixture over, tossing vigorously. Add pasta water a splash at a time to loosen into a creamy, silky sauce.

5

Serve Immediately

Plate right away while steaming hot. Top with extra pecorino, a final crack of black pepper, and serve immediately for the creamiest result.

💡 Chef's Tips

  • Never add the eggs over direct heat — the residual heat from the pasta is enough to cook them gently.
  • Pasta water is the secret — the starch binds everything into a luscious sauce.
  • Use freshly grated cheese, never pre-grated, for the best melt.
  • Traditional carbonara has no cream — the creaminess comes from eggs and cheese.
