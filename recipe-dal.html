Dal Tadka

Creamy yellow lentils cooked to silky perfection, finished with a dramatic sizzling tadka of ghee, cumin, garlic, and dried chillies.

40 Minutes
4 Servings
Easy Difficulty
280 Calories

Ingredients

For the Dal

  • 1 cupToor dal (split pigeon peas)
  • ½ cupChana dal (split chickpeas)
  • 3 cupsWater
  • 1Large tomato, chopped
  • 1Onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tspTurmeric powder
  • 1 tspSalt
  • 1 tspGinger-garlic paste

For the Tadka

  • 2 tbspGhee (clarified butter)
  • 1 tspCumin seeds
  • 4 clovesGarlic, thinly sliced
  • 2Dried red chillies
  • ½ tspKashmiri chilli powder
  • PinchAsafoetida (hing)
  • HandfulFresh coriander, to garnish

Prep Time: 10 min Cook Time: 30 min Total Time: 40 min

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Rinse and Soak the Lentils

Rinse both dals under cold water until clear. Soak for 20 minutes if time allows — this speeds up cooking and improves texture. Drain before cooking.

2

Pressure Cook or Boil Dal

Combine drained lentils, water, turmeric, and salt in a pressure cooker. Cook for 3–4 whistles (or simmer covered in a pot for 25–30 minutes) until completely soft and mushy.

3

Build the Base

In a separate pan, heat 1 tbsp ghee. Sauté onions until golden. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook 1 minute. Add tomatoes and cook until they break down to a thick paste, about 5 minutes.

4

Combine Dal and Base

Add the cooked lentils to the tomato-onion base. Stir well, adjust salt, and add water if needed to reach a creamy, pourable consistency. Simmer together for 5 minutes.

5

Make the Tadka

Heat remaining ghee in a small pan over high heat until shimmering. Add cumin seeds — they should sizzle immediately. Add garlic slices and fry until golden. Add dried chillies, hing, and Kashmiri chilli powder. Swirl for 10 seconds.

6

Finish and Serve

Pour the sizzling tadka directly over the dal — you'll hear a gorgeous crackle. Stir partially to create streaks of red-orange colour. Garnish with coriander and serve with rice or warm roti.

💡 Chef's Tips

  • The tadka (tempering) is what transforms simple lentils into something spectacular — don't skip it.
  • Make the tadka just before serving so the ghee and spices are hot and fragrant.
  • A restaurant trick: add a few drops of lemon juice at the end for brightness.
  • Dal thickens as it cools — add a splash of hot water when reheating.
