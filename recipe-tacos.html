Street-Style Tacos

Juicy spiced meat tucked into warm, doubled-up corn tortillas, topped with fresh salsa, pickled onions, and a squeeze of lime.

30 Minutes
3 Servings
Easy Difficulty
380 Calories

Ingredients

For the Meat

  • 400gBeef skirt or chicken thigh, diced
  • 2 tspCumin powder
  • 1 tspSmoked paprika
  • 1 tspChilli powder
  • ½ tspGarlic powder
  • 1 tbspLime juice
  • 1 tbspOil
  • To tasteSalt & pepper

To Serve

  • 12Small corn tortillas
  • 1White onion, finely diced
  • HandfulFresh coriander (cilantro)
  • 2Limes, cut into wedges
  • 2Ripe tomatoes, diced
  • 1Jalapeño, sliced

Prep Time: 15 min Cook Time: 15 min Total Time: 30 min

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Season the Meat

Combine diced meat with cumin, smoked paprika, chilli powder, garlic powder, lime juice, oil, salt, and pepper. Toss well and marinate for 10–15 minutes.

2

Make Quick Salsa

Combine diced tomatoes, half the onion, coriander, jalapeño, and a squeeze of lime. Season with salt. Set aside — fresh salsa is best made just before serving.

3

Cook the Meat

Heat a heavy skillet or griddle over high heat. Cook meat in a single layer without moving for 2–3 minutes until charred. Flip and cook another 2 minutes. Remove and rest briefly, then chop roughly.

4

Warm the Tortillas

Heat tortillas directly on a gas flame or dry skillet for 30 seconds per side until lightly charred and pliable. Stack them and wrap in a clean cloth to keep warm.

5

Build Your Tacos

Double up the tortillas. Pile on the chopped meat, top with salsa, remaining raw onion, fresh coriander, and jalapeño slices. Squeeze lime generously over everything.

💡 Chef's Tips

  • Always double-up corn tortillas to prevent splitting under juicy fillings.
  • High heat and no stirring creates the best char on the meat.
  • Street tacos are served with simple toppings — resist adding too much.
  • Pickled red onions make a great upgrade: soak onion in lime juice + salt for 20 minutes.
